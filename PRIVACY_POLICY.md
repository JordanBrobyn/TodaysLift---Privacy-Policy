# Privacy Policy for Today's Lift

**Effective date:** September 14, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

Today's Lift is provided by **Jordan Brobyn** ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Today's Lift mobile application for Android and iPhone handles information.

## Summary

Today's Lift does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information with us or with advertisers. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not require an account, and does not use a remote service operated by us. Workout information is stored locally on the user's device.

## Information stored on your device

Today's Lift lets users create and manage information such as a local profile, workout routines, workout history, exercise preferences, notes, custom exercises, and custom exercise media. This information is stored in the app's private storage on the user's device and is not transmitted to us.

Users may choose to export a backup file or a routine file. A routine file contains only the selected routines and any custom exercise definitions they use; it does not include workout history, profile, schedules, or custom exercise media. Exported files are saved to a location selected by the user and are then controlled by the user and by any storage provider or person the user chooses to share them with. Today's Lift does not automatically upload exported files.

## Optional device and Google features

Today's Lift may use Android notifications and a foreground service to provide workout and rest-timer controls. When reminders are on, the app schedules local notifications on the device for planned workout days or after a break. Workout-day reminders are shown pre-selected during first-time setup, where the user can turn them off; they can also be changed at any time in Settings. Notification permission is requested only on supported Android versions and only when a feature that needs it is used. These features do not transmit workout information to us.

After a user has completed several workouts, the app may ask Google Play once to show its rating card. Ratings and reviews are submitted to Google Play under Google's own privacy policy; the app does not learn whether a review was left.

Users may optionally set up voice shortcuts through the Google app or another supported assistant service. When a user chooses this feature, the selected voice phrase and related interaction are handled by that service under the service provider's own privacy policy. We do not receive or store that information on our servers.

On iPhone and iPad, Today's Lift uses the same local notifications for reminders and rest timers, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island during a workout, and App Shortcuts that let users ask Siri to complete a set or control a rest. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. After several workouts the app may ask iOS once to show Apple's rating prompt; ratings and reviews are submitted to the App Store under Apple's privacy policy, and the app does not learn whether a review was left.

The optional **Support development** purchase is processed by Google Play or the App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. The app receives only whether the purchase is owned; we do not receive payment details.

Android text-to-speech may be used to announce workout-command results. Text-to-speech processing is controlled by the text-to-speech engine selected on the user's device and is subject to that provider's terms and privacy practices.

## Feedback

Settings includes an optional **Send feedback** action that opens the user's own email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft contains the app version, the Android or iOS version, and the device model, which the user can edit or remove before sending. No workout data is included. If the user sends the email, we receive the message and the sender's email address and use them only to respond and improve the app.

## Data sharing

We do not receive user information through the app and do not sell or share it. Information leaves the app only when the user deliberately exports a file, selects media for a custom exercise, sends us feedback by email, or uses an optional device or third-party feature described above.

## Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the user's device until the user changes or deletes it, uses the app's delete-all-data feature, clears the app's storage, or uninstalls the app. The app's delete-all-data feature removes its locally stored profile, routines, workout history, preferences, and app-private custom exercise media.

Files that a user has exported to another location are not deleted by deleting app data or uninstalling the app. Users must delete those exported files from the selected location themselves.

## Security

Today's Lift stores app information in the app's private storage on Android and iOS. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, but limiting the app to local storage reduces exposure and the app does not transmit this information to us.

## Children's privacy

Today's Lift is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be posted at this location and its "Last updated" date will be revised.
